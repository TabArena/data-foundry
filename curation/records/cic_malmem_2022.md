Reject check (AI, 2026-10-31): refuted. The SMOTE sentence is in the source, but "the real benign rows cannot be recovered" was a reading, and the file refutes it. On the Kaggle CSV (58,596 rows, the paper's count), `pslist.avg_threads` x `pslist.nproc` is a whole number in 29,298 of 29,298 malware rows but in 4,041 of 29,298 benign rows; 2,010 benign rows pass three such identities (threads, handlers, handles), 1,535 of them in one block at the top of the file. These are the real dumps, and a filter can keep them. Every malware row's `Category` reads `<Category>-<Family>-<sha256>-<n>.raw` (3 categories, 15 families, 2,887 hashes, about 10 dumps each), so a family task never touches the SMOTE rows and can be grouped by sample.
