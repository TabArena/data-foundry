## Comments

https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~jsimonof/AnalCatData/Data/Comma_separated/

Educational datasets from a book. These datasets seem highly constructed, outdated, and trivial. 

It is hard to judge the worth of these dataset for modern tasks.

From looking at several of them, most of them seem to not meet the quality bar for new data. While we appluad the effort and creativity that it tooks to create and gather the data at the time, I would veto to remove all of the datasets from the book as they cleary seem misaligned with today's real-world dataset distribution. Moreover, in several cases it is unclear where the dataset comes from (besides the book)
