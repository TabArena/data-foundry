# simulated-data-for-ml-paper

## Comments

"These are simulated data based on employee turnover data in a real technology company in India"

"Simulated data based on real turnover data at a technology company in India. The real data are analyzed in the 2020 Strategic Management Journal paper titled "Machine Learning for Pattern Discovery in Management Research". These simulated data were created to help readers recreate the analysis while keeping employees in the real data anonymous."
