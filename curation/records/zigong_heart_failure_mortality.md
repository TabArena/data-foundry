**Screening (AI, 2026-10-31):** `TBD -> Yes`, priority 3: 2,008 consecutive heart-failure admissions of one Chinese hospital (December 2016 to June 2019) with 166 EHR attributes and follow-up outcomes at 28 days, 3 and 6 months, published on PhysioNet with a Scientific Data paper; the publisher's target is readmission after discharge (about 39% at 6 months), while death, the scouting run's framing, is rare (about 3% at 6 months).
- Source: Zhang Z, Cao L, Chen R, Zhao Y, Lv L, Xu Z, Xu P, "Electronic healthcare records and external outcome data for hospitalized patients with heart failure", Sci Data 8:46 (2021), doi:10.1038/s41597-021-00835-9 (PMC7865067); the data on PhysioNet, "Hospitalized patients with heart failure: integrating electronic healthcare records and external outcome data" (Zigong Fourth People's Hospital, Sichuan), v1.0 of 2020-09-09, v1.2 doi:10.13026/8a9e-w734 (the version the paper cites), v1.3 of 2022-05-22 doi:10.13026/5m60-vs44 (adds heart-failure drugs to `dat_md.csv`). As published: "168 variables for 2,008 patients" (PhysioNet; 166 in the paper), "aggregated into a single row per hospitalization", blanks for missing values, a variable description file. Admission data: demographics (`admission.ward`, `admission.way`, `occupation`, `visit.times`, gender, age), vitals, height and weight, HF type, NYHA class, Killip grade, GCS, echocardiography, day-one labs, comorbidities; drug categories in `dat_md.csv` without administration times; discharge fields (`DestinationDischarge`: Died 14, Unknown 212, healthcare facility 438, home 1,344; `outcome.during.hospitalization`; discharge date and department). Outcomes "obtained at mandatory follow-up visits at 28 days, 3 months and 6 months" (by telephone when the patient could not come): death and readmission at each horizon, times to death and to readmission, return to the emergency department within 6 months. Retrospective, consent waived, ethics approval 2020-010. Access: registered users who sign the PhysioNet Restricted Health Data Use Agreement 1.5.0 (no credentialing named); a term to note, not a criterion (EV-4).
- Use case: the publisher's: at discharge, the hospital predicts emergency readmission of a heart-failure patient to plan follow-up (PhysioNet Usage Notes: "Data were collected with the goal of developing a model to predict emergency readmission ..."); fit direct for readmission (consecutive admissions, real follow-up). Death of HF patients (the task of `heart_failure_followup_survival`, the scouting run's framing) is a second target with few positives: 29 of 941 analysed patients died within 6 months (3.1%; Sci Rep 2025, the hs-CRP study, PMC12464237), 33 deaths within 90 days among 1,850 (Open Heart 2024, PMC11529686), so roughly 60 deaths in all and about a dozen per test fold (TP-5 risk). Six-month readmission: 773 of 2,002 (38.6%; J Clin Med 2023, PMC9918116). Regime IID, as in the papers and in `heart_failure_followup_survival` (one row per hospitalization of consecutive patients); temporal if an admission date ships unshifted (the paper lists one among the demographics; whether the release keeps it is not stated; SPLIT-7); grouped if a patient has several rows (not stated; `visit.times` counts visits).
- Siblings and duplicates: no other record holds the Zigong data (grep over `curation/records/` by name, PhysioNet slug and both DOIs). `heart_failure_followup_survival` (Yes, BeyondArena: 299 Faisalabad patients of 2015, death within a 4-285 day follow-up, 96 deaths) has the mortality target from another source (not a duplicate, PROV-4); `heart_failure` (No, Duplicate of it); `heart_failure_prediction_dataset` (No, Duplicate of `heart_disease`, a diagnosis task); `synthetic_clinical_tabular_dataset` (No, AHDS); `hospital_readmission` (No, Duplicate of `diabetes_130_us`, readmission of diabetic inpatients); `mimic_iv` and `eicu_collaborative_research_database` (ICU databases of other sources). - Risks for /add-dataset: choose the target and its prediction point (USE-3, USE-5): readmission within 6 months, predicted at discharge (the publisher's goal; the population is then the patients discharged alive, USE-7), or death predicted at admission (rare; drugs, discharge day and destination are then not known yet, USE-4).
